## 2. Cross-cutting patterns

**Works:**
- Vendor-acknowledged **front-loading** (content order matters, end gets cut) — Character.AI [DOC].
- **Separating identity from style**: Candy.ai (personality vs. communication-style layers) and PolyBuzz (brief-intro vs. background-intro) are the only two products found that structurally split "who" from "how."
- **Persistent, never-evicted memory tier**: Kindroid's design ties trait stability directly to *not* being subject to chat-history eviction, rather than to prompt quality.
- Anthropic's **trait-not-rule + self-ranked preference data** loop is the only case in this survey of durability engineered through *training*, not runtime prompting — a different lever entirely from every consumer product above.

**Fails:**
- **Context-window eviction is the named root cause of drift wherever investigated**: Character.AI (~8–9K tokens, ~4K active) [SEC]; Janitor AI (~8–9K, falls out after ~25–30 messages) [SEC]. A shared architectural constraint, not a per-vendor bug.
- **Vendors admit unreliability while shipping anyway**: Character.AI's blog disclaims its own memory feature's reliability at launch [DOC] — a rare, high-value vendor admission.
- **Company denial vs. user reports diverge publicly**: Nomi's founder denies memory loss against years of contrary user reports [SEC] — self-report is not evidence of actual consistency.
- **Freeform blobs correlate with worse reported consistency** (Chai, Janitor AI) vs. more structured wizards (Nomi's locked traits) — suggestive, not a controlled finding.

**Nobody does (checked against the brief's suspicion):**
- **No consumer product publishes pre-deployment behavioral testing, scenario probes, or confidence scores for a character.** Everything resembling it — atomic persona-fidelity metrics, intra-persona consistency scoring, situational-judgment tests — exists **only in academic arXiv papers** (2506.19352, 2510.22170, 2605.28969, 2605.00227). Confirmed absent at product level.
- **No consumer product exposes per-element token accounting in the character-creation UI** ("your personality field costs X tokens"). Existing token tools are generic developer tokenizers, not tied to specific definition fields. Confirmed absent — a genuine white space, distinct from GGBC's own internal Token Breakdown Analysis work.
- **Nobody publishes a methodology** beyond field-level tips (front-load, keep it short, avoid conflicting traits). None of the nine consumer products surveyed offer a decision-framework, conflict-pattern, or voice-marker taxonomy. The closest structured methodology in the entire survey is Anthropic's internal, non-consumer "Claude's Character" essay.

## 3. Implications for a behavioral-definition methodology

1. A persistent/behavioral layer must live **outside the evicted context window**, not just be well-written — Kindroid's design and Character.AI's truncation admission both point to eviction, not authoring quality, as the primary drift driver [DOC/SEC].
2. Front-loading order should be a compilation concern, not creator discipline — Character.AI tells creators to self-manage ordering because the runtime doesn't [DOC].
3. Separate "what the character is" (values/decisions) from "how it talks" (voice markers) as distinct compiled layers — Candy.ai and PolyBuzz are the only precedents, and this maps directly onto the proposed core-values/voice-markers split.
4. Treat vendor consistency/memory claims skeptically and verify empirically — Character.AI disclaims its own feature's reliability, and Nomi's founder's denials contradict multi-year user reports [DOC/SEC].
5. A small, locked, non-conflicting trait set chosen at creation (Nomi: 3 traits + 1 limitation, uneditable) is the only consumer precedent for bounding scope up front instead of an ever-growing freeform blob.
6. Pre-deployment behavioral testing (scenario probes, consistency scoring) has **zero consumer precedent** — differentiated, but unvalidated at product scale outside academic papers.
7. Per-element token cost reporting tied to definition fields has **zero consumer precedent** — same differentiation opportunity, same caveat.
8. Design for graceful degradation under context pressure even at paid tiers — Kindroid's "Cascaded Memory" is a paid-tier *mitigation*, not a fix, and still "may miss some details" [SEC].
9. Don't treat elaborate persona prose as an accuracy/steerability mechanism by itself — a 162-persona study found no measurable gain from expert framing, and another found persona prompting can hurt factual accuracy [SEC, arXiv 2512.05858].
10. Anthropic's explicit "traits, not inviolable rules" framing suggests a behavioral spec should define tendencies/decision-weightings rather than hard scripts, if the goal is drift-resistance under novel inputs [DOC].
